# Personal Site Guidelines

## Aesthetic direction

This site should feel quiet, personal, warm, and editorial. Restraint is the default.

- Let the existing writing and photographs carry the page.
- Prefer simple typography, generous space, muted colors, and thin rules.
- Use one clear visual idea at a time.
- Keep headings and navigation concise.
- Preserve the warm cream and dark themes unless asked to change them.
- Treat reference websites as sources of principles, not templates to imitate.

Avoid adding any of the following unless the user explicitly requests it:

- invented kicker text, category labels, captions, slogans, or explanatory copy;
- decorative numbering such as `001`, coordinates, archive codes, or faux metadata;
- badges, pills, excessive borders, glass effects, glows, gradients, or ornament;
- oversized typography or dramatic layouts that compete with the content;
- extra pages, navigation items, sections, animations, or interactions;
- travel-photography content that belongs in `glasshousegalary`.

## Working method

- Preserve the user's language; do not rewrite or expand it without permission.
- Make the smallest change that achieves the requested result.
- Keep distinct aesthetic ideas in separate commits so they can be reverted independently.
- Never merge, delete branches, or rewrite history unless explicitly requested.
- Preview meaningful visual changes in both light and dark themes.
- Check desktop and narrow layouts before considering a visual change complete.
